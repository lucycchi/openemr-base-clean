# Document fixture sources

Rule: synthetic data, templates, published samples with fictional names,
and public de-identified patient data are all acceptable. Nothing identified,
and nothing obtained outside the dataset's own access terms: a source that
needs a PhysioNet credential or a DUA is used only after that access is
granted, and its terms are recorded on its row. A fixture built from
de-identified data keeps the values but never the free text verbatim, and
gets an invented name, DOB and dates matched to a seed patient. Every file
under this directory has a row here with its origin, its terms, and what was
changed. Files not listed here are generated by `generate.py` (synthetic).

Status legend: `candidate` (found, not vetted), `vetted` (checked by Lucy
Chi: fictional data, terms allow use), `chosen` (a fixture is built from it),
`rejected` (with the reason).

## Filled forms and scanned documents

No public corpus of filled patient intake forms is known. Kaggle sets titled
"medical form OCR" or "lab report OCR" are small and of unclear provenance,
rejected for the same reason as the Scribd copies. FUNSD
(https://guillaumejaume.github.io/FUNSD/) and RVL-CDIP are scanned-form
datasets with layout ground truth but non-medical; only useful for
sanity-checking OCR and anchoring on messy real scans before the hand-filled
intake. The route is therefore: a Synthea or MIMIC patient → answers drawn from
their meds, allergies and conditions (reworded, never copied from a note)
→ typed into a blank form above → one copy printed, hand-filled and scanned.

## How a chosen source becomes a fixture

1. Fill the form (or edit the sample) with invented answers tied to a seed
   patient so the chart-vs-document cases are real (for example, write
   "stopped lisinopril" for a patient whose chart still lists it).
2. Save as PDF (text layer). For at least one intake form, also print it,
   fill it by hand, and scan or photograph it to PDF: handwriting is the
   honest stress test.
3. Write `<name>.truth.json` (every clinical field with page and row) and,
   for anchor-mode cases, record `<name>.model.json` once from the real
   model.
4. Add the row above with status `chosen` and the changes made.
