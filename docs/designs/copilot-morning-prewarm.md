## Problem Statement

The Co-Pilot briefing is generated lazily when a clinician opens a chart. On a
cache miss that is one OpenAI round-trip on the critical path: 3-6 s p50, up to
15 s p99 at 10 VUs (`tests/load/results/20260917T0245Z-*`), about $0.10 per
cold brief. The cache (`copilot_briefing_cache`, keyed by sha256(facts_hash |
Prompt::VERSION | model), with pid stored alongside) already makes re-opens free, but nothing ever
populates it before the first open of the day. The appointment book knows
exactly whose chart will be opened today and by whom. The run happens at
06:00 on the clinic day itself (`--date=today`); `--date=tomorrow` exists
only for the manual evening test.

## What Makes This Cool

The cache key is a hash of the facts themselves, so a pre-warmed entry can
never be served over changed facts; it simply misses. That turns the usual
cache-warming hazard (stale warmed data) into a non-issue and turns the design
into a hash-match optimizer: make the 6 a.m. facts identical to the 9 a.m.
facts as often as possible, and measure the rate. The receipt table makes every
miss explainable. Because the sweep's traces are tagged `source=prewarm`, it
also becomes a natural place for a later prompt canary (see Follow-ups); v1
only adds the tag.

## Constraints

- Cache key design is frozen: no TTL-based staleness, no "may be stale" labels
  (v1 decision D20). Change what is assembled, never what is hashed.
- Facts are viewer-dependent: `FactAssembler` drops encounters whose
  `sensitivity` the viewer's ACL cannot see (`FactAssembler.php:41`).
- `background_services` only ticks while a user is logged in; after-hours work
  needs a host cron.
- Deployed on a droplet as flex image + bind-mount (`docker/development-easy`
  shape); no custom image.
- OpenAI RPM and MySQL must not be flooded by the sweep (load tests show
  OpenEMR saturates at ~3 req/s regardless of VUs).
- Rejected during the session, one line each:
  - A live "Today" block (reason for visit, vitals, symptoms) in the AI
    summary: vitals and reason are already on the dashboard; the Co-Pilot's
    value is the history diff. Summary stays history-only.
  - Warm on check-in as a trigger (D10 b): tight window, no clean OpenEMR
    "arrived" event, 8:00 burst. Kept as the first event-driven seam.
  - Registering in `background_services`: still needs the cron, adds a lease
    layer the console command does not need in v1.
  - Bare `bin/prewarm.php`: OpenEMR already boots site + DB for console
    commands via `bin/command-runner` + `CommandRunnerFilterEvent`.

## Premises (agreed)

1. Goal: a measured warm-hit rate at chart open. Cache key unchanged.
2. History facts exclude encounters dated on or after the target-date
   boundary. Today's encounter (created at check-in) is the visit being
   prepared for, not history. The boundary is the same in both paths: start
   of the target day in `gbl_time_zone`, whether the opener has an encounter
   selected (start of that encounter's day) or not (start of "today" by the
   clock). The job assembles as the appointment's provider
   (`pc_aid` -> `users.username` -> `AclAuthorization`) with the clock pinned
   to the appointment date and no current encounter, so the hash matches
   through check-in and rooming. Vitals are not a fact category, so rooming
   never touches the hash. A viewer with a different sensitivity ACL than the
   scheduled provider misses; accepted for v1 and logged as `viewer_differs`.
3. Trigger: a Symfony Console command registered by the module, run by host
   cron at 06:00 site-local. Writes: `copilot_briefing_cache` and one receipt row
   per appointment.
4. The panel labels the narration with when it was generated, never "current
   as of": a warm hit is verified against live facts at display time, so
   "generated 6:02 AM today · matches chart as of now" is the honest label;
   a live regeneration reads "generated just now". The per-category delta
   line ("N new since 6:02 AM") is a follow-up, gated on the miss log.
5. Bounded cost: only appointments on the target date with a patient and a
   non-cancelled status; skip when the entry for the current hash exists;
   sequential in v1; flock so overlapping crons cannot double-run.
6. Real overnight changes (new lab, med, allergy, problem) still miss and
   regenerate. That is correct behaviour, not a defect.

## Cross-Model Perspective

Independent cold read (Claude subagent, no conversation context; same harness,
weigh as one model's view):

- Coolest unconsidered version: the sweep is a free eval harness; verifier and
  omission stats already flow to Langfuse, so a prompt/model change can be
  canaried overnight on real charts. Folded in via `source=prewarm` trace tag.
- What excites the user: "any facts-changing write re-warms that patient;
  nightly becomes the sweep" - the panel should be reactive, not merely fast.
- 50% off the shelf: `bin/command-runner` + `CommandRunnerFilterEvent`,
  `symfony/console`, `symfony/process` already vendored. Adopted.
- Challenged premise #2: check-in creates today's encounter and
  `FactAssembler.php:55-60` emits every encounter newer than the prior visit
  as a fact, so the hash would miss for exactly the patients who show up.
  Verified in code; premise #2 revised as above. The subagent's alternative
  ("normalize the hash to ignore today's encounter") was rejected: hashing
  something other than what is rendered reintroduces stale-serve bugs.
- Weekend order: command by hand for one pid; then the warm_hit/miss log;
  then cron; build the delta line only once the log shows hashes match often
  enough to matter. Adopted: the delta line is in Follow-ups, not v1.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Bare warm (S; human ~1.5 d / CC ~1.5 h)
Command + cron + assembler change + generated-at label; hit rate read from
Langfuse only. Rejected: cannot say why a miss happened.

### Approach B: Warm with a receipt (M; human ~3 d / CC ~3 h) - chosen
A plus a per-appointment receipt row, warm_hit/miss reasons logged at open,
`source=prewarm` traces, flock. Delta line, catch-up pass and concurrency
are listed as follow-ups gated on the receipts.

### Approach C: Rolling just-in-time warm (S-M)
Cron every 15 min warms appointments starting within 90 min. Rejected for v1:
96 runs/day, per-patient timestamps are harder to explain, and it still needs
the miss log to prove it beats 06:00. Kept as a `--window` selector seam on
the same command.

## Recommended Approach: B

### Components (module `oe-module-clinical-copilot`)

1. **`FactAssembler` boundary rule.** Introduce one `boundary(): array{DateTimeImmutable,int}`
   = `[startOfDay(D), 0]` where D is the selected encounter's date when
   `$currentEncounterId` is set, else `clock->now()`, in the site's PHP
   zone. **Probed 2026-09-17 (dev container):** `ServiceContainer::getClock()`
   is `SystemClock::fromSystemTimezone()`, i.e. `date_default_timezone_get()`,
   which `interface/globals.php` sets from `gbl_time_zone` when that global
   is configured and leaves at the PHP default (UTC here) when it is empty.
   So the command builds `FixedClock` in `date_default_timezone_get()` after
   globals load, never `$GLOBALS['gbl_time_zone']` directly (empty on this
   site). Both open paths and the command then share one zone, which is what
   the hash match needs. Runbook: set `gbl_time_zone` on the droplet so
   "start of today" is clinic-local rather than UTC.
   `priorEncounter()` uses this boundary (today: it uses `[$e->date, $e->id]`
   of the current encounter, which differs from the null path when
   `form_encounter.date` carries a time or two same-day encounters exist).
   Drop encounters with `[$e->date, $e->id] >= $boundary` before emitting
   `Encounter` facts and before building `$hiddenEncounterIds` for the lab
   filter. Bump `Prompt::VERSION`. Tests in `FactAssemblerTest`: (a) a
   same-day encounter produces no `Encounter` fact and does not shift
   `prior_visit`; (b) open-with-today's-encounter-selected and
   open-with-null-encounter yield the same `facts_hash`; (c) the hash is
   identical before and after the same-day encounter row is created.

2. **`BriefingPipelineFactory`** (`src/BriefingPipelineFactory.php`): extract
   the five lines in `ChatController::pipeline()` (OpenAiClient, DbBriefingCache,
   Verifier, OmissionGuard, NarrationPipeline) into a factory taking
   `(Config, AssembledFacts, PatientId, string $correlationId, StepRecorder)`,
   so the controller and the command share one wiring. No behaviour change;
   existing controller tests cover it.

3. **`PrewarmCommand`** (`src/Command/PrewarmCommand.php`, registered from
   `Bootstrap` via `CommandRunnerFilterEvent::setCommand()`), invoked as
   `php bin/console copilot:prewarm --site=default --date=tomorrow|today|YYYY-MM-DD
   [--pid=N] [--dry-run] [--force]` (`--concurrency` is a follow-up).
   - Kill switch: `Config::prewarmEnabled` from env `COPILOT_PREWARM_ENABLED`
     (default off). When off the command prints "pre-warm disabled on this
     site" and exits 0 without touching the schedule, the cache or OpenAI;
     `--force` bypasses it for a one-off manual run. The switch stays off on
     the droplet until the user turns it on (decision 2026-09-17).
   - Selector: `openemr_postcalendar_events` rows where `pc_eventDate = :date`,
     `pc_pid <> ''`, `pc_apptstatus NOT IN ('x', '?')` (cancelled / no-show),
     `pc_aid` joined to `users.username`, grouped by `(pc_pid, pc_aid)` so a
     patient with two slots is warmed once per provider.
   - Per row: `new FactAssembler(new OpenEmrChartSource(),
     new AclAuthorization($username), FixedClock::startOfDay($date, $tz))`
     `->assemble($pid, null)`, then `BriefingPipelineFactory` ->
     `NarrationPipeline::brief()`. `FixedClock` is a tiny `ClockInterface`
     value object in the module; `$tz` is `date_default_timezone_get()` after
     `bin/console` loads globals. **Probed 2026-09-17:** with no session user,
     `AclMain::aclCheckCore($section, $value, 'admin')` returns true for
     `patients/med`, `encounters/notes`, `sensitivities/high` and false for an
     unknown username; the explicit-username path works from the CLI. The
     dev site has only `admin`; re-check a non-admin provider on the droplet.
     Also: `RootCliGuard` refuses UID 0, so the cron line must run as the web
     user (`docker exec -u apache`). Correlation id per row; Monolog processor
     already attaches it; add a `source=prewarm` attribute on the Langfuse
     trace and a `prewarm_run_id`.
   - Concurrency: sequential in v1 (80 patients x ~5 s = ~7 min, inside the
     06:00-08:00 window). `--concurrency` via a `symfony/process` pool of
     child `--pid` invocations is a follow-up. Flock on
     `sites/<site>/documents/copilot/prewarm.lock` so overlapping crons
     cannot double-run.
   - Exit code non-zero if any row errored; summary line: scheduled, warmed,
     already_cached, skipped, errored, total ms, total model calls.

4. **Receipt table** `copilot_prewarm` (module `sql/install.sql`):
   `id, run_id CHAR(26), site, target_date DATE, pc_eid INT, pid BIGINT,
   provider_username VARCHAR(255), facts_hash CHAR(64), cache_key CHAR(64),
   prompt_version VARCHAR(32), model VARCHAR(64), fact_lines_json JSON
   (one `id|category|value` line per fact, the same lines `FactSet::hash()`
   hashes), status ENUM('warmed','already_cached','skipped','error'),
   duration_ms INT, model_called TINYINT(1), correlation_id CHAR(26),
   created_at DATETIME`. No PHI beyond fact ids
   (which are `Service#id.field` references, same as the cache) and the fact
   values already stored in the cache row. Single index
   `(target_date, pid, provider_username)`.

5. **Chart-open lookup** (`ChatController::brief()`): after assembling facts,
   select the `copilot_prewarm` row for `(today, pid)` whose
   `provider_username` equals the opener, else the latest row for
   `(today, pid)`. Log one event `copilot.warm` with `{result: hit|miss,
   reason: no_row|hash_drift|viewer_differs|prompt_version|model_changed, new_fact_ids:
   [...], gone_fact_ids: [...]}` to Monolog + a Langfuse score. The diff is
   over fact lines (`id|category|value`), not ids, because a hidden encounter
   can shift `since` and flip a medication's category with an unchanged id.
   Reason rules, evaluated in order: `no_row` if no receipt; `prompt_version`
   if `receipt.prompt_version !== Prompt::VERSION`; `model_changed` if
   `receipt.model !== config model`; `viewer_differs` if the opener's username
   differs from `provider_username` AND every changed line's id is an
   `EncounterService#` or `ObservationLabService#` id or a line whose only
   change is category (the effects the sensitivity filter can have);
   otherwise `hash_drift`.
   One indexed query; must stay under 10 ms on the hot path.

6. **Panel label** (`PanelPayload::briefing()`, `panel.js`): payload gains
   `generated_at` (cache row `created_at`). Render "AI summary generated
   6:02 AM today · matches chart as of now" when the narration came from the
   cache; "AI summary generated just now" when it was generated in this
   request. Contract: `chat.briefing.response.schema.json` gains the optional
   field; regenerate fixtures.

7. **Cron on the droplet: documented, not installed.** Nothing in the deploy
   path adds the crontab entry; installing it is a separate manual action,
   and `COPILOT_PREWARM_ENABLED` must also be set for it to do anything. The
   line to document (runbook or deploy notes): `0 6 * * 1-5 docker exec <openemr> php
   /var/www/localhost/htdocs/openemr/bin/console copilot:prewarm --site=default
   --date=today >> /var/log/copilot-prewarm.log 2>&1` using `docker exec -u
   apache` (the CLI refuses root). The host crontab runs
   in the host's zone; set the line so 06:00 is site-local (`gbl_time_zone`).

### Cost and load envelope

80 scheduled patients, ~5 s per cold brief, sequential: ~7 min, ~$8/day
worst case (all cold), ~$0 on quiet days (all `already_cached`). Sequential
is far below the load-test saturation point (3 req/s).

### Alerting (extends `clinical_copilot_week1/ALERTS.md`)

- `prewarm_errored > 0`, or no `copilot_prewarm` row with today's
  `target_date` by 06:30 on a weekday (a cheap SQL check the existing
  `health.php` can expose as `prewarm_last_run`) -> page.
- Warm-hit rate over the previous clinic day < 50% for 3 days -> ticket; read
  the reason histogram before acting.

## Follow-ups (deliberately out of v1)

All gated on one clinic week of `copilot.warm` receipts.

- **Delta line**: "generated just now · 1 new result since 6:02 AM", listing
  `new_fact_ids` by category under the narration. The receipt already stores
  what is needed; only the panel changes.
- **07:30 catch-up pass** or a `--window=90m` selector (approach C's seam):
  choose based on how many `hash_drift` misses carry a lab id.
- **Check-in re-warm** (D10 b): listener on appointment status -> `@`
  Arrived; choose based on drift misses in the last hour before open.
- **`--concurrency`** child-process pool if the sequential run exceeds the
  window.
- **Prompt canary**: bump `Prompt::VERSION` before the sweep and compare
  `source=prewarm` verifier/omission scores in Langfuse before clinic.

## Open Questions

1. Multi-provider clinics where a nurse or MA opens the chart before the
   provider: their ACL may differ (sensitivity). The `viewer_differs` reason
   will size this; if large, warm per distinct ACL profile rather than per
   provider.
2. `pc_apptstatus` codes are site-configurable (`list_options.apptstat`); the
   exclusion list should be read from `list_options` where `toggle_setting_*`
   marks cancelled/no-show, or exposed as a module config. Hard-coded `('x',
   '?')` is acceptable for the demo site only.

## Success Criteria

- On the droplet, after `copilot:prewarm --date=today` at 06:00, opening a
  scheduled patient after front-desk check-in logs `copilot.warm result=hit`
  and the brief renders in the cache-hit envelope (< 1 s module time at 10 VUs).
- `FactAssemblerTest`: a same-day encounter produces identical `facts_hash`
  before and after its creation, and with or without that encounter selected.
- Warm-hit rate visible in Langfuse as a score split by `source`; reason
  histogram available for misses.
- Sweep traces distinguishable from interactive traces on the dashboard.
- `phpstan` level 10 clean for all new files; no baseline entries added.

## What shipped (2026-09-18)

All six steps below landed on `audit`, each test-first, each verified live
in the dev container (commits `5c2ec1d`, `730a090`, `7c29539`, `0c4e3a2`,
`bab4b34`, `e141779`; probe results in `6b19efb`, kill switch in
`52d7c4d`, docs in `507da1c`). Deviations from the plan as written:

- The `encounter` fact category is no longer emitted at all: it was only
  ever fed by same-day encounters, which the boundary rule now excludes.
  Enum case and contract entry kept; removing them is a contract change for
  a later pass.
- `WarmOutcome` checks prompt version and model *before* the hash: a model
  change with identical facts is a miss (the model is in the cache key), and
  the first draft got that order wrong until a test caught it.
- The run signal is a new `public/prewarm.php` (+ `prewarm.response`
  contract) rather than a field on `health.php`, whose contract promises
  liveness only.
- Per-patient failures catch `\RuntimeException | \LogicException`, not
  `\Throwable`: the repo's PHPStan rule forbids swallowing `\Error`, and a
  real PHP error should abort the sweep.
- `openemr-cmd down` drops the dev volumes (demo data, module registration,
  dev deps), so the dev stack was re-seeded twice during the work.
- The cron line is documented (docker/vps/README.md) but not installed, and
  `COPILOT_PREWARM_ENABLED` is unset on the droplet, per the user's
  2026-09-17 decision.

Not yet done from the plan: the live evals have not been re-run since the
`Prompt::VERSION` bump to `2026-09-18.2` (EVALS.md asks for that on every
bump), and nothing has been pushed or deployed.

## What I noticed about how you think

- You cut your own idea. You proposed a live "Today" block, then said "maybe
  there's no point in having vitals and reason for the visit part of the ai
  summary." Most people defend the thing they just suggested.
- You asked "are you saying that the results from the nightly run is thrown

  from one that works on a clinic day.
- You moved the run from 2 a.m. to "the morning before clinic opens (like at
  6am)" and asked for a visible timestamp. Both are operator instincts:
  fresher data, and a label a clinician can trust.
