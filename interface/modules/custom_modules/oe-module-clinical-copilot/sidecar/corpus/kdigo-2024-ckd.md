# KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline (summary)

## Definition and staging
Chronic kidney disease is defined as abnormalities of kidney structure or function present for more than 3 months with implications for health. It is staged by GFR (G1 90 or higher; G2 60 to 89; G3a 45 to 59; G3b 30 to 44; G4 15 to 29; G5 below 15 mL/min/1.73m2) and by albuminuria (A1 below 30 mg/g; A2 30 to 300 mg/g; A3 above 300 mg/g urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio). An eGFR below 60 for more than 3 months, or albuminuria of 30 mg/g or more, meets the definition regardless of cause.

## Estimating GFR
Use a validated creatinine-based equation without a race coefficient (CKD-EPI 2021) for initial assessment; add cystatin C when creatinine-based eGFR is likely to be inaccurate (extremes of muscle mass, certain drugs) or when a more accurate estimate would change management, for example around the 60 mL/min threshold or before drug dosing decisions.

## Monitoring frequency
Monitoring frequency follows risk: low risk (G1 to G2 with A1) once a year; moderately increased risk once a year; high risk twice a year; very high risk (for example G4, or G3b with A3) three to four times a year. A sustained decline in eGFR of more than 5 mL/min/1.73m2 per year, or a confirmed drop of 25 percent or more in category, is progression and warrants review of causes.

## Referral to nephrology
Refer for eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73m2, persistent albuminuria of 300 mg/g or more, rapid progression, persistent hematuria with albuminuria, refractory hypertension or recurrent hyperkalemia, or when the cause is uncertain.

## Treatment principles
Blood pressure target of systolic below 120 mm Hg when tolerated in adults with CKD; an ACE inhibitor or ARB for CKD with moderate or severe albuminuria; an SGLT2 inhibitor for CKD with eGFR of 20 or higher with albuminuria, diabetes or heart failure; a statin or statin plus ezetimibe for adults 50 and older with CKD; avoid NSAIDs and adjust renally cleared drugs, including metformin, to eGFR.
