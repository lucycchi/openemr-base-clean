# USPSTF screening recommendations (summary)

## Prediabetes and type 2 diabetes (2021, grade B)
Screen adults aged 35 to 70 years who have overweight or obesity for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes; offer or refer patients with prediabetes to effective preventive interventions. Screening is with fasting plasma glucose, A1C, or an oral glucose tolerance test; a reasonable interval is every 3 years.

## Lipid disorders and statin use for primary prevention (2022, grade B and C)
For adults aged 40 to 75 with one or more cardiovascular risk factors (dyslipidemia, diabetes, hypertension, smoking) and an estimated 10-year cardiovascular risk of 10 percent or higher, initiate a statin (grade B). For the same age group with risk factors and a 10-year risk of 7.5 to under 10 percent, selectively offer a statin (grade C). Evidence is insufficient for adults 76 and older.

## Hypertension in adults (2021, grade A)
Screen adults 18 and older for hypertension with office blood pressure measurement, and obtain measurements outside the clinical setting (home or ambulatory monitoring) to confirm the diagnosis before starting treatment. Screen every year for adults 40 and older and for those at increased risk; every 3 to 5 years for adults 18 to 39 at lower risk with a normal prior reading.

## Colorectal cancer (2021, grade A and B)
Screen adults aged 45 to 75 for colorectal cancer (grade A for 50 to 75, grade B for 45 to 49); selectively screen adults 76 to 85. Options include colonoscopy every 10 years, annual fecal immunochemical testing, or other tests at their recommended intervals.

## Breast cancer (2024, grade B)
Biennial screening mammography for women aged 40 to 74.
